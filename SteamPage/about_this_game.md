# About This Game

**Critter Cove is a cozy co-op zoo builder with bite.** Explore a massive, living
wilderness, catch the critters that roam it, and raise them into a zoo that hums with
income — solo, or with up to three friends dropping into your world to help.

### 🤝 Build a Zoo With Friends

Open your cove to up to three friends over Steam. Visitors roam your world as their own
avatar, hunt wild critters alongside you in real time, and send gift animals straight to
your collection. It's your zoo — you decide what guests can do.

[image: two avatars in the same zoo, one collecting income, one mid-catch]

### 🌍 Explore a Vast Procedural World

Your home plot sits at the heart of a seamless, procedurally generated wilderness — no
loading screens, no edges in sight. Wander through forests, arctic flats, savanna,
jungle, desert, ocean coast, and stranger biomes still, each with its own critters to
find. Mark fast-travel waypoints, zoom the camera, and dash across the map to wherever
the rare ones hide.

[image: wide shot showing several biomes blending together with wild critters scattered around]

### 🎯 Catch Critters That Fight Back

Catching isn't a guaranteed click. Enter catch mode and fill the capture ring by closing
the distance — but every species runs its own playbook. Zigzaggers juke, bursters dash,
circlers orbit, vanishers hide, and the bold ones charge, lob projectiles, or stalk and
lunge to knock you back and break your catch. The rarest critters won't come quietly —
you'll have to catch them more than once.

[image: catch mode UI — capture ring filling over a fleeing critter, screen-shake/impact effect visible]

### 🧬 Breed Strange Hybrids

Lead your critters on a physics-based follow chain back to the nests, pair them up, and
wait. From a catalog of 100+ species you'll unlock crossbreeds and themed exotics — odd,
delightful hybrids you can only get by experimenting with who you put together.

[image: nest with two animals + a freshly hatched hybrid offspring]

### 💰 Grow an Idle Empire

Every critter earns while it lives in your cove. Coins and DNA Helix accrue over time —
click a full critter to collect, level it up, and rank up duplicates for bigger payouts.
Place animals on income pedestals to bank rewards even faster while you're away, and
unlock food structures to fuel it all. Play actively and optimize, or let it tick along
and come back richer.

[image: HUD showing coins / DNA Helix counters and a row of animals on pedestals]

### ✨ Built to Feel Good

Soft isometric pixel art, biome colors that blend into one another, swaying grass, and
satisfying collect-pops. Tune the look with fullscreen filters — scanlines, pixelate,
sepia, and more — and feel every big catch through camera shake and impact effects.

[image: small gallery strip — three thumbnails showing the art style / different filters]
